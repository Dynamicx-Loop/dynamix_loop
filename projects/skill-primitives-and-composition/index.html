External research to investigate

These are research candidates, not integrations.

  • GPC: Generative Pretrained Controllers learn a discrete vocabulary of motor skills and reuse it for new control tasks. We want to study how to compose those skills for specific tasks.
  • SMP: Score-Matching Motion Priors use pretrained motion models as reusable rewards that guide how policies move. They do not select or sequence skills.
