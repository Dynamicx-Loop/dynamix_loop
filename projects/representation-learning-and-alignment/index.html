External research to investigate

These are references for our System 1 research, not methods we have integrated.

  • SADL: Adds a constraint only when the encoder loses distinctions that the output needs. The aim is to prevent representation collapse without changing the main training objective.
  • INTACT: Learns from offline trajectories with recorded actions. It encodes the current state and a desired outcome, then uses that goal representation to generate actions.
